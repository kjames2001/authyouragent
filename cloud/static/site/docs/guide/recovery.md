# Lost phone and account recovery

## Set this up before you need it

Recovery needs two things you must set up while you still have access:

1. **A verified phone number.** Go to **Account → Phone number**, enter your number, and type the code you receive by text message.
2. **Recovery codes.** Go to **Account → Recovery codes** and tap **Create recovery codes**. You get 10 one-time codes, shown once. Write them down or save them in a password manager, somewhere other than your phone.

Creating new codes cancels the old ones.

## If you still know your password

You don't need recovery. Sign in on the new phone with your email and password, then add a passkey on that phone (**Account → Add passkey**).

Your old phone keeps receiving requests until you remove it under **Account → Devices that get requests**.

## If you can't sign in

You need both your verified phone number (a SIM that receives texts to it) and one unused recovery code.

1. On the sign-in screen, tap **Lost access?**
2. Enter your email and your verified phone number. If they match, a code arrives by text message. For privacy, the screen looks the same whether or not they matched.
3. Enter the text-message code, one recovery code, and a new password.

When this succeeds:

- you are signed in
- **every passkey, device and session on the account is removed**, so a lost or stolen phone is cut off
- requests that were waiting are cancelled
- that recovery code is used up; the screen tells you how many are left

Then add a passkey on your new phone and turn notifications back on.

## Limits

- The text-message code is valid for 10 minutes and allows 5 attempts.
- Recovery can be started at most 5 times per hour.

## If you have neither

Without your password, a verified phone and a recovery code, the account can't be recovered. This is deliberate: anyone able to recover it another way could take it over. Your agents stay under your control only as long as you keep one of these.
